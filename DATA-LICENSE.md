## Release restrictions

- Public availability is not, by itself, proof of permission to republish or redistribute a dataset or asset.
- The project links to official member directories and retrieves data at runtime. This does not establish that every field, compilation, image, or API response can be republished under an open data license.
- Do not add copied official photographs, logos, PDFs, or bulk datasets unless the applicable permission or license has been verified and recorded.
- Keep official-source URLs and retrieval timestamps visible; do not describe live records as a frozen historical snapshot.
- Before distributing a downloadable data snapshot or making rights-sensitive commercial reuse, confirm applicable terms with the source owner and record the decision here.
