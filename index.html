Methodology

This interface is a presentation layer. Source-backed facts and derived calculations must be distinguished, and parliamentary data should always be treated as time-bounded.
Current datasetThe application loads the repository's previous embedded dataset from a preserved legacy page. It is used for continuity of the existing explorer while the data layer is being reworked.
Facts vs calculationsMember attributes such as name, party, state and constituency are treated as source-backed fields. Rankings, averages, percentages, scores and distributions shown here are calculated by the application.
FreshnessThe UI does not claim that the embedded dataset is live. Future refreshes should expose both retrieval date and dataset as-of date in the interface.
Preferred referencesDigital Sansad Lok Sabha member information and Rajya Sabha member directory are the preferred starting points for validation. Reuse of third-party photographs, logos, PDFs or compilations requires a separate rights check.
Scope directionThe product architecture is designed to grow from a Lok Sabha explorer into a Parliament-wide explorer covering both Houses, sessions, legislation, constituencies and reusable structured datasets.
